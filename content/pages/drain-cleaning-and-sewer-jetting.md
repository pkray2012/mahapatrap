## Why does my bathroom drain keep blocking?

A bathroom drain keeps blocking when hair and soap build up inside the floor trap and the pipe after it. Old GI pipes also narrow with rust over time. Cleaning only the top of the trap gives short relief. The trap and pipe both need cleaning.

Other common causes:

- **Grease in kitchen pipes:** Oil cools and sticks inside the pipe.
- **Poor slope or a sunken pipe:** Water and waste sit in the pipe. This needs pipe work, not just cleaning.
- **Choked outside chamber:** The block may be outside, not in the bathroom.

If the same drain keeps blocking, we check the pipe and tell you the cause. A broken or leaking pipe is a repair job. See our [leak and blockage repair](/services/leak-and-blockage-repair/) service.

## Should I use chemical drain cleaners?

Use chemical drain cleaners with great care, and never on a drain that is fully blocked. Strong acid or caustic cleaners can burn skin and eyes. They can also harm old pipes and joints. If the drain stays blocked, the chemical sits in the pipe. Always tell the plumber if you have already poured one in.

Safer steps and warnings:

- **Pull out hair** from the floor trap with a gloved hand.
- **Never mix two drain cleaners.** Some mixtures give off harmful gas.

## Blocked drain or full septic tank?

If only one sink or toilet is blocked, the problem is usually that drain pipe. If every toilet and floor trap in the house is slow or gurgling at once, the main line or the septic tank may be the cause. We check and tell you which.

## Simple habits that prevent blockages

- **Fit a jaali (mesh cover)** on floor traps to catch hair.
- **Wipe oily kadai and plates** before washing. Never pour used oil in the sink.
- **Do not flush** wipes, sanitary pads or cotton.

### Why does my bathroom drain smell even when it is not blocked?

A smell from a drain that flows well often means the floor trap has dried out. The water in the trap stops sewer gas from coming up. Pour a mug of water into unused traps every few days. If the smell stays, the trap may be broken or the line may need cleaning. We can check it for you.

### How do you clear a toilet that is fully blocked?

We clear the pan and the pipe after it, then flush a few times to check the flow. If the block is further down, we check the outside chamber too. Toilet blockage removal starts from ₹999. Stop flushing until the plumber arrives, so the pan does not overflow.

### What should I avoid before the plumber arrives?

Stop using the blocked sink, toilet or bathroom so water does not overflow. Do not pour acid or more chemicals in. Do not keep flushing a blocked toilet. Clear the space under the sink and around the floor trap. If you can, send a short video on WhatsApp so the plumber knows what to expect.
