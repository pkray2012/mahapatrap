## Motor and pump problems we fix

Our motor repair work covers the pipes and valves between your sump and overhead tank:

- **Foot valve faults:** A stuck or leaking foot valve, so the motor will not pick up water.
- **Air in the suction line:** The motor hums but no water comes. We find and seal the air leak.
- **Non-return valve problems:** Water runs back to the sump when the motor stops.
- **Leaking pump joints:** Drips at the suction and delivery joints of the motor.
- **Sump to overhead tank line:** Cracked, leaking or blocked delivery pipes, in CPVC, UPVC or PPR.
- **Pressure line leaks:** Loose joints between a pressure pump and your taps.

## Why is my motor running but not filling the tank?

A motor that runs but does not fill the tank usually has one of five causes. The sump water is too low, air is trapped in the suction pipe, the foot valve is stuck, a valve is closed, or the delivery pipe leaks. Switch the motor off first. Running it without water can damage the pump.

- **Water comes, then stops:** Low sump water, or an air leak at a joint.
- **Weak flow at the tank:** A leaking or blocked delivery pipe, or a half-closed valve.
- **Motor will not start:** Often electrical. Check the switch and MCB first.

## Why does my overhead tank overflow?

An overhead tank overflows when nothing stops the water once it is full. On a line fed from the supply, a stuck or broken float valve is the usual cause. On a motor-filled tank, the motor is left running or the water level controller has failed. A blocked overflow pipe makes it spill over the top.

We fix the float valve, inlet and overflow pipe. For a controller or wiring fault, we tell you if an electrician is needed.

## Water motor and tank repair FAQs

### Why does water flow back into the sump when the motor stops?

That usually means the non-return valve on the delivery line is faulty, or the foot valve is not closing. Water then drains back, and the motor has to refill the line each time. We check both valves and replace the faulty one. Water pump repair starts from ₹399, and parts are extra.

### Do you repair motor winding and electrical faults?

Our work is on the plumbing side of the motor: valves, pipes, joints and connections. If the fault is in the winding, capacitor, switch or wiring, we tell you whether you need a motor mechanic or an electrician. Call +91 9668875352 and describe the problem first.

### What is a foot valve and why does it fail?

A foot valve sits at the bottom of the suction pipe in your sump. It lets water in and stops it from falling back, so the motor stays filled with water. It fails when dirt, leaves or sand jam it, or when its flap wears out. The motor then loses water and cannot pick up.

### Is it safe to run a water motor without water?

No. A motor running without water heats up quickly and can damage the pump and its seals. If no water comes, switch the motor off. Check the sump level and the valves, then call us. Restarting it again and again will not fix an air lock or a stuck foot valve.

### Can you repair a submersible pump in my sump?

Yes, on the plumbing side. We check and fix the delivery pipe, joints and non-return valve of a submersible pump in a sump. If the pump itself has a winding or electrical fault, we tell you who to call. For submersible pumps inside a borewell, see our borewell pump and pipe repair page.

### What should I check before calling for motor repair?

Check that the switch and MCB are on and the sump has enough water. Make sure every valve on the line is open. Look for wet patches near the motor and along the pipe. Then call or WhatsApp +91 9668875352. A short video of the motor running helps us understand the problem.
