## How do I know a pipe is leaking inside the wall?

The usual signs are a damp patch that keeps coming back, peeling paint, loose or hollow-sounding tiles, and low water pressure at one tap. A simple home check helps. Close every tap and watch your overhead tank or water meter. If the level still drops, water is escaping somewhere.

Other signs to look for:

- The water motor runs more often than before
- Water marks on the ceiling below a bathroom
- A musty smell or fungus near the skirting

If you see these, send a photo or short video on WhatsApp. We look at it and tell you what to check before the site visit. For a dripping tap or a running flush tank, see [tap and pipe leak repair](/services/leak-and-blockage-repair/) instead.

## Should I replace leaking GI pipes with CPVC in an old house?

If old GI lines in your house leak in more than one place, replacing that section with CPVC is often the better choice. Patching one joint on a rusted line can lead to the next leak nearby. We check the line at the site visit and tell you whether a repair or a replacement makes sense.

Signs an old line is near the end:

- Brown or rusty water when you first open a tap
- Low pressure across several taps
- Leaks that return after a repair

CPVC pipework handles hot and cold water, so it suits geyser lines. UPVC works for cold water lines. PPR suits both. If you plan to redo the whole bathroom anyway, see our [bathroom renovation plumbing](/services/bathroom-plumbing-renovation/) page.

## Concealed pipeline FAQs

### Can you fix a concealed pipe leak without breaking many tiles?

We try to open only the part of the wall or floor needed to reach the leak. How much depends on where the leak is and how the line runs. We explain this at the free site visit, so you know before work starts. Keep a few spare tiles from your original lot if you have them.

### Who fixes the tiles and plaster after the pipe repair?

Mahapatra Plumbing Services does the plumbing work: finding the leaking section, repairing or replacing it, and checking the joints. For re-tiling and plaster, we work with your tile or civil mistri. We tell you when the line is ready, so they can close the wall or floor.

### Is CPVC pipe good for hot water from the geyser?

Yes. CPVC pipe handles both hot and cold water, so it suits geyser lines in bathrooms and kitchens. UPVC is meant for cold water lines only. PPR also works for hot and cold water. At the site visit, we suggest the pipe that fits your line and budget, and share the part price before fitting.

### Can you change a concealed line into an open line?

Yes. If a concealed line keeps leaking, we can reroute it as an open line along the wall. Open lines are easier to reach later, but they show on the wall. Concealed lines look neater but are harder to repair. We explain both options at the free site visit and put the choice in your written estimate.

### Is every damp patch on a wall caused by a pipe leak?

No. A damp patch can also come from rain seepage, a gap in the bathroom tile joints, or a leaking drain or floor trap. That is why we check before opening any wall. If the cause is not a water supply pipe, we tell you. Send a photo on WhatsApp to +91 9668875352 first.

### Can you add a new water point using CPVC pipe fitting?

Yes. We add new points for a tap, geyser, washing machine or RO purifier by extending your existing line with CPVC pipe fitting. The point can be concealed or open. You get a written estimate before work starts, and the work has a 90-day workmanship warranty. Parts follow the brand warranty.
