## Geyser repair: problems we fix

We fix these plumbing-side geyser problems:

- **Leaking inlet or outlet:** Dripping joints, worn washers and loose connection pipes.
- **Valve and PRV problems:** A pressure release valve that drips all the time, sticks or is missing.
- **Tank leaks:** Water from the body or bottom of the geyser. We tell you if repair makes sense.
- **Low hot water flow:** Blocked inlets, scaled pipes or a half-closed valve.
- **Scaling from hard water:** White deposits that cause noise, slow heating and less hot water. We descale during a service, where the model allows.

For leaks in other pipes, taps or the flush tank, see our [pipe leakage and blockage repair](/services/leak-and-blockage-repair/) page.

## Why is my geyser not giving hot water?

A geyser usually stops giving hot water for one of four reasons: a power fault, a failed heating element or thermostat, heavy scale in the tank, or a mixing problem in the pipes. First check the switch and MCB. If power is fine, call Mahapatra Plumbing Services. We check the geyser and fix plumbing-side causes. For a failed element or thermostat, we tell you to call an electrician or the brand's service centre.

Common signs and what they often mean:

- **Water only lukewarm:** Scale on the element, or a thermostat fault.
- **Hot water runs out fast:** Scale has filled part of the tank. Or the geyser is too small for your family.
- **Hot at the geyser, cold at the tap:** A mixer or valve problem, or a long pipe run.

## How often should a geyser be serviced?

Service your geyser once a year, ideally in October or November before winter starts. If your home uses borewell or hard water, get it serviced every six months. ## Geyser safety tips

- **Switch off the power first.** Turn off the switch and the MCB before you touch or check the geyser.
- **Never run it dry.** Open the inlet valve and let water flow from the hot tap before switching on. Do this after every water cut too.
- **Do not block the safety valve.** A few drops while heating can be normal. A steady flow is not.
- **Smell burning or feel a shock?** Switch off at the MCB and call an electrician.

## Geyser repair FAQs

### What is included in a ₹499 geyser service?

### Do you repair geyser heating elements and thermostats?

No. Heating element, thermostat and wiring faults are electrical, and we do not replace those parts. We check the geyser and tell you whether it needs an electrician or the brand's service centre. We handle the plumbing side, such as leaks, valves, pipe connections and scale from hard water. Call +91 9668875352 and describe the problem first.

### Can you install a geyser I bought online?

Yes. We fit geysers bought online or from a local shop anywhere in Bhubaneswar. Installation starts from ₹699 for labour. Connection pipes, valves and other parts are extra if the box does not include them. Check first if your brand offers free installation, as some brands do.

### Which size geyser do I need for my bathroom?

It depends on how many people bathe one after another and whether you use a shower or a bucket. An instant geyser of a few litres suits a kitchen sink or quick hand wash. A storage geyser suits bathing. Bigger families and bucket baths need a bigger tank. Check the space on your bathroom wall too.

### Why is my geyser leaking from the bottom?

A leak at the bottom often comes from a loose inlet or outlet joint, a dripping pressure release valve or a worn drain plug. These are usually repairable. If water comes from the tank body itself, the tank may be rusted through. We check it and tell you if repair makes sense or a new geyser is better.

### Should I repair or replace my old geyser?

Repair makes sense for leaking joints, a faulty valve or scale build-up. Think about replacing it if the tank body leaks or shows rust, or if repairs keep coming back. We check it and give you a written estimate, so you can decide.
