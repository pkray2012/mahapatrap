## Geyser repair: problems we fix

We fix these plumbing-side geyser problems:

- **Leaking inlet or outlet:** Dripping joints, worn washers and loose connection pipes.
- **Valve and PRV problems:** A pressure release valve that drips all the time, sticks or is missing.
- **Tank leaks:** Water from the body or bottom of the geyser. We tell you if repair makes sense or a new geyser is better.
- **Low hot water flow:** Blocked inlets, scaled pipes or a half-closed valve.
- **Scaling from hard water:** White deposits that cause noise, slow heating and less hot water. We descale during a service.

For leaks in other pipes, taps or the flush tank, see our [pipe leakage and blockage repair](/services/leak-and-blockage-repair/) page.

### What about electrical faults?

Heating element, thermostat and wiring faults are electrical work. Your geyser may trip the MCB, show no light, or stay cold with power on. We check it and tell you if it needs an electrician or the brand's service centre.

## Why is my geyser not giving hot water?

A geyser usually stops giving hot water for one of four reasons. These are a power fault, a failed heating element or thermostat, heavy scale in the tank, or a mixing problem in the pipes. First check the switch and MCB. If power is fine, call Mahapatra Plumbing Services and we will check the cause.

Common signs and what they often mean:

- **Water only lukewarm:** Scale on the element, or a thermostat fault.
- **Hot water runs out fast:** Scale has filled part of the tank. Or the geyser is too small for your family.
- **Hot at the geyser, cold at the tap:** A mixer or valve problem, or a long pipe run.

## Geyser safety tips

- **Switch off the power first.** Turn off the switch and the MCB before you touch or check the geyser.
- **Never run it dry.** Open the inlet valve and let water flow from the hot tap before switching on. Do this after every water cut too.
- **Do not block the safety valve.** A few drops while heating can be normal. A steady flow is not.
- **Smell burning or feel a shock?** Switch off at the MCB right away and call an electrician.
