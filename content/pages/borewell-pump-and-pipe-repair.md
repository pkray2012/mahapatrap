## Why is my borewell giving less water?

A borewell usually gives less water for one of these reasons: a leaking or cracked pipe, a faulty non-return valve, a clogged or worn pump, a power problem, or a fall in the water level. Mahapatra Plumbing Services checks the pipes, joints and valve first. If the pump or motor is the problem, we tell you.

Common signs:

- **The tank takes longer to fill than before:** A leaking joint or damaged pipe may be sending water back down.
- **Water starts, then slows or stops:** The water level may be falling. Or air may be entering through a leak.
- **Water drains back when the motor stops:** This usually points to the non-return valve.

## Borewell repair FAQs

### Do you need to pull out the submersible pump to repair a borewell?

Not always. A leak at the borewell head, a fault in the line to your tank or a valve near the top can be fixed without lifting. A leaking column pipe joint, a valve fitted just above the pump or a pump check needs the pump lifted. Mahapatra Plumbing Services tells you which one applies after inspection.

### What is a non-return valve in a borewell?

A non-return valve lets water flow up to your tank but stops it from falling back down when the motor switches off. When it fails, water drains back and the pipes empty. The motor then takes longer to deliver water. Mahapatra Plumbing Services checks and replaces faulty non-return valves on existing borewells in Bhubaneswar.

### Why does my borewell motor run but no water comes out?

Switch the motor off first, because running it without water can damage the pump. Common causes are a broken or disconnected pipe in the borewell, a stuck valve, a pump fault, or a water level that has fallen below the pump. Call Mahapatra Plumbing Services on +91 9668875352. We check the pipes and valve and tell you the cause.
