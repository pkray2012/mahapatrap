## Why do older houses in Sailashree Vihar keep leaking?

Older houses in Sailashree Vihar often still run on galvanised iron (GI) water lines. GI rusts from the inside as it ages. The bore narrows, joints weaken and pinhole leaks start inside the walls. You notice it as weak flow, brown water after a gap, or a damp patch that keeps coming back.

Other signs in an independent house or duplex:

- Weak flow upstairs even when the overhead tank is full.
- The overhead tank empties faster than your family uses water.

If a floor was added later, check where the new pipe joins the old GI. Mixed joints are a common weak point.

It starts with a free site visit. The plumber traces where your GI lines run, from the overhead tank down to each bathroom and the kitchen. Then you get a written estimate. You can change the whole house together, or begin with the bathroom that gives the most trouble.
