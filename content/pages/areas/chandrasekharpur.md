## What plumbing problems come up in Chandrasekharpur apartments?

In apartment blocks, the usual calls are leaking flush tanks, worn jet sprays and taps, choked kitchen sinks, and damp patches on the ceiling of the flat below. Stacked bathrooms mean one small leak can trouble two families. We trace the source before breaking any tile. Other flat jobs:

- **Washing machine on the balcony.** Inlet tap and drain pipe fitting. See [bathroom and kitchen fittings](/services/bathroom-and-kitchen-fittings/).
- **Low water in one bathroom only.** Often a choked angle valve or wall line. Hidden leaks go to [concealed pipe leakage repair](/services/pipeline-and-concealed-plumbing/).
- **Geyser fitting after shifting in.** A geyser fitted or moved in a new flat.

## What do independent houses in Chandrasekharpur and Damana need most?

Independent houses run on their own sump, motor and overhead tank. So most house calls here are about water supply: a motor that runs but does not fill the tank, an overflowing tank from a stuck float valve, or a leaking line from the sump. Some houses also have an existing borewell pump.
