## What plumbing problems do Patia homes usually have?

Patia homes mostly need small fixes: a dripping tap, a flush that keeps running, a choked bathroom floor trap or a leak at the geyser inlet. Apartment blocks add leaks that reach the flat below. Independent houses add sump, motor and overhead tank work. We take jobs in homes near Patia railway station and the Kanak Durga Temple.

### Rented flats

Fittings in a rented flat can go years without a check. Worn washers, loose jet spray hoses and tired flush kits are the usual suspects. We repair or replace them, and share the part price before fitting.

### Apartment blocks

In an apartment, a bathroom leak often shows up as a damp patch on the ceiling below. We trace it from the bathroom above. If the pipe runs inside a wall or floor, [concealed pipeline repair](/services/pipeline-and-concealed-plumbing/) starts with a free site visit and written estimate.

### Independent houses

A house in Patia usually has its own sump, motor and overhead tank. When the motor runs but the tank stays empty, we check the foot valve, joints and roof line. Houses on an existing borewell can book [borewell pump and pipe repair](/services/borewell-pump-and-pipe-repair/).
