## Infocity plumbing: common questions

### Do you need a visitor pass to work inside an Infocity office building?

If your building gives visitor or contractor passes, please arrange one for the plumber before the slot. Share the name and number of the person who will meet the plumber at reception. This saves time at security, so work can start sooner.

### Can you close the water to one office washroom without stopping the whole floor?

It depends on how the lines are laid. If the washroom has its own valve, we close only that one. If not, we tell you before shutting a bigger line. We can also fit a separate valve, so the next repair does not disturb the whole floor.

### Can you fit the water inlet and drain for an RO purifier in our office pantry?

Yes. We fit and repair the water inlet, tap point and drain line for an RO purifier in office pantries and home kitchens. Filters and electrical parts are a job for the purifier brand's service team. Send a photo of the pantry wall on WhatsApp first.

### Our office pantry sink in Infocity keeps choking. How do we stop it?

Keep tea leaves, food bits and oil out of the sink waste, since they settle in the trap. Fit a steel strainer and empty it into a bin. If the sink still chokes, we clear the trap and check the slope of the drain line. Drain unclogging starts at ₹349.
