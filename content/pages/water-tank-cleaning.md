## How often should a water tank be cleaned?

Most homes in Bhubaneswar should get the overhead tank and sump cleaned at least twice a year. Clean them sooner if the water looks muddy, smells bad, or leaves sand in the bucket. Book a cleaning after the monsoon too, because rainwater and mud can enter a sump with a loose lid.

How often your tank needs cleaning depends on a few things:

- **Water source.** Borewell water often carries more sand and iron than supply water.
- **The lid.** A loose or broken lid lets in dust, leaves and insects.
- **Number of users.** Hostels, PGs and apartments draw more water, so dirt settles faster.

## What are the signs your water tank needs cleaning?

The main signs are muddy or yellowish water, a bad smell from the taps, and sand in buckets. Slimy green growth on the tank walls is another sign. Insects or larvae in the water mean the tank needs cleaning soon.

Sand can also block a jet spray or shower head. Yellow or reddish water can also come from iron in borewell water or from old pipes. If the water stays coloured after the tanks are cleaned, ask us to check the pipeline.
