# Privacy Policy

[BREADCRUMB: Home > Privacy Policy]

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This page explains what information Mahapatra Plumbing Services collects on mahapatraplumbing.in, why we collect it, and who receives it. It also explains your rights under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act). We are a local plumbing business at District Center, Chandrasekharpur, Bhubaneswar, Odisha 751016.

## What we collect

**When you use our forms.** The Quick Quote and Book a Visit forms ask for your name, phone (WhatsApp) number, area and a short note about the problem. When you press send, the form opens WhatsApp with this message filled in. Your details go only into that WhatsApp message. We do not store them on our website.

**When you call or WhatsApp us.** We see your phone number and anything you choose to send, such as photos or videos of the problem.

**When you browse the site.** Analytics and ad tools record how the site is used. This includes pages viewed, buttons tapped (for example "Call" or "WhatsApp"), how far you scroll, and which page a lead came from. These tools also receive basic technical details by their nature, such as your IP address, device type, browser and approximate location. We do not send your name, phone number, address, email or message text to these tools.

## Why we use it

- To reply to you, give an estimate and book a visit.
- To send the plumber to the right address and finish the job.
- To raise a bill or GST invoice when you ask for one.
- To see which pages and buttons help people reach us, so we can improve the site.
- To measure and improve our online ads.

## Cookies, pixels and the services we use

The site uses small scripts and cookies from these services.

| Service | What it does | Personal data we send |
|---|---|---|
| Google Tag Manager | Loads the other tools below | None |
| Google Analytics 4 | Counts visits, pages and button taps | None |
| Microsoft Clarity | Heatmaps and anonymous session recordings to fix layout problems. All form fields are masked | None |
| Google Ads | Measures which ads lead to calls and WhatsApp chats | None |
| Microsoft Advertising (UET) | Measures which Bing ads lead to enquiries | None |
| Meta Pixel (Facebook and Instagram) | Counts page views, call and WhatsApp taps, and form leads from ads | Only with your choice, see below |
| WhatsApp (Meta) | Carries your form message and chats to us | The details you type or send |
| Google Maps | Shows our location map | Your IP address, when the map loads |

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site still works without them.

## The optional "Use my details to improve ads" box

Our forms may show an optional box: "Use my details to improve ads". It is unticked by default. We use it only if you tick it yourself.

If you tick it, the Meta Pixel sends Meta your phone number, first name, and city, state and country (Bhubaneswar, Odisha, India). These values are scrambled (hashed) in your browser before they leave your device. Meta uses them to match the enquiry to a Facebook or Instagram account, so our ads reach the right people. We never send your address, email or problem description this way.

If you leave the box unticked, nothing personal is sent to Meta. A basic "lead" count without your details may still be recorded.

## Who receives your data

- **WhatsApp and Meta:** your form messages and chats, and the hashed details above only if you tick the box.
- **Google:** site usage and ad measurement data, without your name or number.
- **Microsoft:** Clarity and Bing ad measurement data, without your name or number.
- **Our website host:** standard server logs, such as IP address and pages requested.

These companies may process data on servers outside India under their own privacy policies. We do not sell your personal data to anyone.

## How long we keep it

We keep your details as long as needed to answer and serve you, or as the law requires. For example, bills and GST records are kept for the period the law sets. Analytics and ad data are kept according to the settings of each service.

## Your rights under the DPDP Act

You can ask us to:

- **Access** a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it.
- **Correct** or update details that are wrong or incomplete.
- **Erase** your details when they are no longer needed for the purpose you gave them.
- **Withdraw consent** at any time, including for the ads box.
- **Nominate** someone to use these rights for you if you die or cannot act yourself.

To use any of these rights, email amareshmohapatra143@gmail.com. Give the phone number you used, so we can find your messages.

## Children

This site is meant for adults. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to contact us for you.

## Grievances

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, email amareshmohapatra143@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy grievance". We will reply as soon as we can. If you are still not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
