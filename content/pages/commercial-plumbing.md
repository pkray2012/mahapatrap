## What our commercial plumbing services cover

A business building has more washrooms and longer pipe runs than a home. Our commercial plumbing services cover:

- **Hotels and guest houses.** Hotel plumbing services: leaking taps, running flush tanks, geyser fitting and blocked room drains.
- **Offices.** Office plumbing services for washrooms, pantry sinks and RO purifier points, including IT offices in Infocity.
- **Hostels and PGs.** Hostel and PG plumbing near KIIT and Patia: blocked toilets, jet sprays and flush faults.
- **Shops, restaurants and cafes.** Kitchen sink lines and drains, hand-wash basins and staff washrooms.
- **Apartment societies.** Apartment plumbing maintenance for common lines: overhead tanks, sumps, motor lines and shared drain lines.
- **Small commercial buildings.** New water supply and drain lines, and washroom fit-outs.

We work with CPVC, UPVC and PPR pipes. We supply parts, or fit the ones you buy.
