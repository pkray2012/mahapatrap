## Is sewerage work the same as drain cleaning?

No. Drain cleaning clears a blocked line that is otherwise sound. Sewerage work repairs, replaces or lays the line itself. If a line blocks again and again, the pipe may be broken, sagging or laid without enough slope. Then cleaning alone will not fix it.

If the line only needs clearing, book [drain cleaning and blockage removal](/services/drain-cleaning-and-sewer-jetting/). If the septic tank is full, book [septic tank cleaning](/services/septic-tank-cleaning/). We tell you which job it is before any work starts.

## How do I know my sewer line is broken?

Common signs of a broken sewer line are a sewage smell in the compound and wet or sinking soil along the line. Slow drains in every bathroom at once is another. Backflow at the lowest floor trap is a late sign. If you see two of these together, the line itself may need repair.

- **Smell outside.** A sewage smell near the chamber, the wall or the garden.
- **Wet or sinking ground.** Damp patches or dipping soil along the line.
- **Every drain slow.** All bathrooms drain slowly, not just one.
- **Backflow.** Dirty water comes up at the lowest floor trap or chamber.
- **Repeat blockages.** The same line blocks again soon after cleaning.
